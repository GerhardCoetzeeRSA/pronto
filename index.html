How it works

Six steps.
Zero queue.

Customers browse and pay from home. Your kitchen gets the order instantly. They collect when it's ready — no waiting, no guessing.

01
📱
Browse the menu
Full menu on any smartphone browser — no app install. Works on 3G. Fast on any network.
02
💳
Pay online
Card, Instant EFT, SnapScan or Zapper. Every major South African payment method, built in.
03
🍳
Kitchen gets notified
Order appears on the kitchen screen instantly. Staff confirm with one tap. Timer starts. Customer is informed.
04
⏱
Live countdown
Customers see a real-time prep timer. Push alerts fire at every stage — no more calling to ask "is it ready?"
05
🎉
Collect with order code
Customer shows a bold order number at the till. In and out in seconds — hot food in hand.
06
⭐
Rate, tip, return
Customers rate their experience and tip the team. You capture satisfaction data and build loyalty — automatically.

of the journey.

Kitchen-triggered alerts reach customers at every stage — order received, prep started, ready to collect.
📊
Owner analytics
Revenue, order volume, peak hours, popular items and satisfaction scores — per branch or chain-wide.
🏪
Multi-branch management
Central menu control with per-location customisation. Add a branch special in seconds.

SA-native payments
PayFast, Ozow, SnapScan, Zapper — every method SA customers already trust and use daily.
